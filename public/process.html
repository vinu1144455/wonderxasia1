END-TO-END EXECUTION

From Indian Mandis to Global Ocean Ports

A transparent, step-by-step export journey that guarantees lot traceability, precision Sortex optical grading, compliant packaging, and punctual container shipping across 38+ countries.

STANDARDIZED EXPORT LIFECYCLE

The 6-Stage Export Execution Pipeline

Every consignment follows a strict protocol ensuring laboratory compliance, customs clearance, and containerized integrity.

01
📋

Technical Specification & CoA Alignment

We align on purity grades (e.g. 99% Machine Clean vs 99.5% Sortex), moisture ceilings, volatile oil minima, mesh particle size, and ASTA colour value. Comprehensive Certificates of Analysis (CoA) and test protocols are shared for pre-contract buyer approval.

02
🚜

Origin Cluster Procurement & Traceability

Our field sourcing team purchases crop lots directly from verified agricultural mandis (Unjha for cumin, Nizamabad for turmeric, Guntur for chillies, Kota for coriander). Each lot is logged with harvest date and regional origin.

03
⚙️

Sortex Optical Cleaning & Metal Detection

Raw spice lots pass through multi-stage pre-cleaners, aspirators, gravity separators, and Buhler Sortex optical colour sorters to eliminate foreign matter, weed seeds, and discoloured grains. Inline rare-earth magnets ensure zero metallic contamination.

04
📦

Export Packing, OEM & Nitrogen Flushing

Packaging into 25kg / 50kg food-grade PP bags with moisture-resistant polyethylene liners, multi-wall Kraft sacks, or custom private label retail pouches. Nitrogen flushing prevents oxidative rancidity during maritime transit.

05
🔬

Laboratory Testing & Export Certification

Representative batch specimens are drawn for GC-MS testing in NABL accredited labs. Issuance of Phytosanitary Certificate from the Ministry of Agriculture, Certificate of Origin (COO), Commercial Invoice, and Packing List.

06
🚢

Container Stuffing & Ocean Bill of Lading

Dry container stuffing at Nhava Sheva (JNPT) or Mundra with silica gel desiccants to prevent condensation sweat. Cargo fumigation (Methyl Bromide / Phosphine), customs sealing, and prompt bill of lading (B/L) dispatch.

FLEXIBLE TRADE TERMS

Standardized Export Contracts Under ICC Incoterms 2020

Wonderxasia provides flexible quotation structures tailored to your procurement preferences and freight forwarding contracts:

FOB (Free On Board) — Nhava Sheva / Mundra Most Popular

Buyer books ocean freight; Wonderxasia handles all inland logistics, customs clearance, and port loading.

CIF / CFR (Cost, Insurance & Freight) — Destination Port Turnkey

All-inclusive pricing to your port of discharge (e.g. Jebel Ali, Hamburg, Rotterdam, New York, Singapore).

Air Freight (CIP) — BOM / DEL International Airports Expedited

Ideal for urgent high-value spice orders (Saffron, Green Cardamom, spice oleoresins, premium lots).
