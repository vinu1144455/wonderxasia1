CORPORATE PROFILE & IDENTITY

Wonderx Innovations Pvt Ltd: Powering India's Global Export Footprint

Operating internationally under the trademark export brand Wonderxasia, Wonderx Innovations Pvt Ltd is an Indian multi-sector export conglomerate headquartered in India. We connect indigenous agricultural mandis, agro-nutritional processors, and precision automotive engineering clusters directly with overseas commercial distributors and institutional buyers across 38+ countries.

By bypassing speculative brokers and trading intermediaries, we provide full origin traceability, Sortex optical machine cleaning, certified pre-shipment laboratory inspections (SGS, Geo-Chem, NABL), and priority maritime container dispatch through India's premier western gateway ports — Nhava Sheva (JNPT) and Mundra.

OPERATIONAL ARCHITECTURE

Six Pillars of Our Export Framework

From raw mandi harvest to vessel loading at Nhava Sheva (JNPT) and Mundra, every stage adheres to rigorous standard operating procedures.

01
🌱

Mandi-Direct Origination

Direct procurement across Gujarat (Unjha Cumin), Andhra Pradesh (Guntur Chilli), Telangana (Nizamabad Turmeric), and Kerala (Tellicherry Pepper), eliminating intermediaries and ensuring pure farm origin.

02
⚙️

Sortex Optical Cleaning

Advanced multi-deck aspiration, destoning, gravity separation, and high-resolution Buhler optical colour sorters achieving 99.5% to 99.8% European and US machine-clean purity thresholds.

03
🔬

NABL / SGS Lab Verification

Pre-shipment parametric testing for Aflatoxins (B1/G1), zero synthetic dyes (Sudan, Metanil Yellow), organochlorine pesticide residues, Salmonella, E. Coli, and heavy metals.

04
📦

Export Packaging & OEM

Food-grade 25kg/50kg PP sacks with moisture barrier liners, multi-ply Kraft paper bags, big jumbo totes, or retail stand-up zip pouches featuring private-label branding and multilingual labeling.

05
📜

Complete Regulatory Docs

FSSAI, Spices Board of India registration, APEDA registration, Ministry Phytosanitary Certification, Chamber of Commerce Certificate of Origin, and container fumigation endorsements.

06
🚢

Maritime Logistics & Port Stuffing

Dry container stuffing oversight at Nhava Sheva (JNPT) and Mundra with container desiccants, customs sealing, prompt ocean Bill of Lading transmission, and competitive ocean freight coordination.
