RIGOROUS VERIFICATION

Export Quality & Laboratory Framework

Ensuring zero adulteration, chemical safety compliance, and conformity with Codex Alimentarius, European Union (EU), and US FDA import thresholds across every Indian agricultural export container.

PARAMETRIC SAFETY TESTING

Multi-Stage Laboratory Inspection Protocols

Indian spices offer remarkable volatile flavor profiles, but international commerce mandates zero chemical residues. Every harvested batch undergoes rigorous parametric validation in NABL-accredited third-party laboratories (SGS, Geo-Chem, Edward Food Research):

  • ✓
    Aflatoxin B1 & Total (G1/G2):
    Tested via HPLC to satisfy stringent European Union thresholds (< 5 ppb B1, < 10 ppb total).
  • ✓
    Sudan Dye & Metanil Yellow:
    Certified non-detection of industrial synthetic coloring agents (Sudan I, II, III, IV) verified via LC-MS/MS.
  • ✓
    Heavy Metals Limit:
    Lead (Pb), Cadmium (Cd), and Arsenic (As) maintained well within Codex Alimentarius standards.
  • ✓
    Microbiological Ceilings:
    Salmonella negative in 25g, E. Coli < 10 cfu/g, and Total Plate Count (TPC) conforming to food-grade specs.
